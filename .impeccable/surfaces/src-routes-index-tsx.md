# Home workspace

Mode: Operate

Audience: people preparing images for sharing who want local metadata cleanup or an evidence-based AI-signal scan.

Job: choose one of two tasks, drop in a JPEG, PNG, or WebP, understand the result, and either download the cleaned file or read the scan evidence.

Constraints: all image processing stays in the browser; the AI scan never claims certainty from absent metadata; the cleaner never promises to defeat platform classifiers.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A careful picture desk presents source, claim, and supporting notes as one evidence folder; it refuses the generic centered dropzone plus benefit-card landing page.

OWN-WORLD: Manila and paper-white leaves, near-black ink, editorial green, and one rust stamp form a physical evidence folder. Square tabs, ruled fields, crop-pencil marks, and verification slips replace generic soft cards.

STORY: The user chooses Clean metadata or Scan AI signals, sees that the file remains local, loads an image, then follows an explicit evidence trail to a cleaned download or an honest likelihood result.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact masthead and two folder tabs sit above a split work surface. The source image occupies roughly two-thirds of the width; the right column is a verification slip with file facts, evidence rows, confidence language, and the primary action at its foot. The signature interaction is the folder leaf changing task: the active leaf advances, its evidence rail redraws once, and the resulting stamp resolves into view without hiding content by default.

FORM: Evidence Folder, the top-ranked grounded direction and Impeccable's pick from direction seed adc006dd.
